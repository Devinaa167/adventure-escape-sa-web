About Adventure Escape SA

History

Established in 2024 by Liam Daniels in the Western Cape to unite guided outdoor experiences on one simple booking platform.

Vision

To become South Africa's premier platform for eco-tourism and outdoor group adventures.

Mission

Delivering safe, professionally guided experiences that build teamwork, fitness, and environmental appreciation.

Goals

Offer transparent group discounts, expand outdoor package varieties, and maintain seamless web and mobile access.
